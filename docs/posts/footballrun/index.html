I found that teams tyipcally pass the ball much more on third and medium than they do run the ball. In fact, more often there’s an incomplete pass than there is a run play. That’s because those third and six rush plays don’t average out to get the first down, it’s on average a yard short of the mark. That’s a encouraging sign for teams who are looking to run the ball on third and six and then go for it on fourth down, as the average would lend to them getting just one yard short of the first down.
